The Kubex Connector is a self-contained, Windows-based application. It allows you to perform automated, scheduled data collection from your vCenter Servers and transfer that data via HTTPS to Kubex where data analysis is performed. Optionally, you can connect to vRealize Operations (vROps) to obtain historical workload data.

<Note>
You cannot perform a vROps-only audit.
</Note>



Though, vROps is very useful for collecting historical workload data, Kubex also requires access to your vCenter in order to collect configuration data and hierarchy information, including information on datastores, storage, etc. Additionally, if you plan to use action automation, access to the vCenter is required.

If you are only using a vCenter connection, then workload and configuration and hierarchy information are collected from each vCenter Server. Only the current day's data (yesterday) is collected and depending on your policy setting you may need to collect a few days of data before reliable analysis results are generated.

For added security, the Kubex Connector polls and pushes data to the remote Kubex server, no data is ever pushed from the Kubex server. The connector allows you to collect vCenter data within your firewall without Kubex connecting to your internal network.

Review the [prerequisites](./Connector_Prerequisites) before starting, to ensure your system meets the minimum requirements.
